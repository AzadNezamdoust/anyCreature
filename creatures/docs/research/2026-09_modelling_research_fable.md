## 0. One-paragraph answer

The owner's eye is right and the cause is representational, not tuning: every anyCreature surface is a ring loft closed by caps and rammed into its neighbours, and nothing in the spec can make it anything else. Humans who make the look the owner wants (Synty / Quaternius / Grant-Abbitt low poly) do the opposite: **one mesh, ~300-2 000 triangles, edge loops only where the form changes, limbs extruded out of the torso so they share vertices, flat shading, 4-8 flat colours, and a deliberate touch of asymmetry** - and they get there by looking and nudging. The research says LLMs are good at the *program-shaped* parts (part list, extrude/loop-cut sequence, materials, rig placement) and bad at the *eye-shaped* parts (proportions, left/right, depth). So: **LLM writes a short box-modelling program in a real modelling kernel (Blender via pip `bpy`, renders on CPU here), deterministic tools do mirror/topology/rigging/export/measurement, and a VLM drives a few critique->edit rounds.** I built that loop in ~1 h: a 580-tri rigged, animated, vertex-coloured wolf GLB that passes `harness/glbcheck.mjs` and `harness/outline.py` ("orbit holds, the head reads") and next to `example/wolf_beauty.png` looks like an asset a person made (`$S/compare_engine_vs_boxmodel.png`).

## 1. How humans do it

### 1a. Direct low poly (Synty / Quaternius / PS1 / mobile) - no sculpt
Sources: Grant Abbitt "Model any low poly animal" https://www.classcentral.com/course/youtube-blender-5-for-beginners-model-any-low-poly-animal-detailed-follow-along-guide-510491 ; Envato ninja https://design.tutsplus.com/tutorials/creating-a-low-poly-ninja-game-character-using-blender-part-1--cg-16132 ; https://www.onlinedesignteacher.com/2019/01/low-poly-character-modelling-part-1.html ; https://astropad.com/blog/how-to-model-a-low-poly-dog-in-blender-3-0/ ; https://tohawork.com/en/zeromodelling ; https://sundaysundae.co/how-to-make-low-poly-look-good/
1. **Reference first**: front + side orthographic sheet loaded on the X-Z / Y-Z planes; the model is matched view by view. For creatures "profile shots from two angles, one the side" is treated as mandatory.
2. **Cube + Mirror modifier (Clipping on)**; model one half.
3. **Extrude the big masses along the body** (front face -> neck/head, back face -> rump/tail); a segment only where the form changes (chest, waist, hips, neck, skull, brow, muzzle).
4. **Loop cut (Ctrl-R), then scale/slide the ring** so the belly drops, the chest swells, the neck narrows. Edge sliding for rounded forms is explicit in Abbitt's method.
5. **Legs are extruded out of the torso's bottom faces** (thigh -> shank -> paw), rings scaled, knee forward, hock back. The leg shares the torso's vertices: no intersection, no seam.
6. **Ears/tail/horns are extrusions** from the skull/rump; eyes, teeth, claws are small primitives joined at the end.
7. **Flat shade (or auto-smooth by angle), apply mirror, then break symmetry** (tilt an ear, curl the tail, shift a shoulder).
8. **Colour**: material slots (one flat colour per region), vertex paint, or a gradient/palette atlas where each face's UV sits on one pixel (https://polycount.com/discussion/231536/what-are-these-types-of-texture-maps-called , https://itch.io/blog/797457/optimization-of-3d-texturing-for-mobile-games-definition-of-the-gradient-texturing-process-and-analysis-of-its-efficiency-and-performance-in-engine). Same idea in all three: few colours, no painted detail, facets do the shading.
9. **Rig**: armature at the joints + Automatic Weights (heat), a few shoulder fixes; ~10 min at this density.
Budgets: PS1 <= ~500 tris (https://medium.com/@kent_edoloverio/how-to-make-ps1-style-in-blender-9e3d719f80c9); stylised indie 1-5 k; "spend the budget where the silhouette matters - head, hands" (https://my3dmodels.com/blogs/3d-world/low-poly-character-design-tips-and-tricks).

### 1b. Sculpt -> retopo (stylised AAA / hand-painted)
Blockout (spheres/ZSpheres or Blender **Skin modifier**: vertices+edges = skeleton, per-vertex radius, "Create Armature" for free; https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/generate/skin.html , https://gamedev.net/tutorials/visual-arts/using-the-skin-modifier-in-blender-to-quickly-model-creatures-r5004) -> DynaMesh sculpt -> ZRemesher/QuadriFlow or manual retopo with animation loops -> bake -> paint (https://80.lv/articles/001agt-stylized-character-art-workflow-from-blockout-to-render , https://www.thegnomonworkshop.com/workshops/modeling-a-stylized-character-for-games). The Skin modifier IS anyCreature's representation (skeleton + radii -> surface) and every source says the same of it: "quick base mesh for sculpting; for a low-poly creature there is no substitute for placing the polys by hand; you always tweak afterwards." The engine ships the Skin-modifier output.

### 1c. Topology rules that read as hand-made and deform well
(https://thundercloud-studio.com/article/topology-for-low-poly-game-characters/ , http://wiki.polycount.com/wiki/Topology , https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/collect/tips-for-creating-topology-for-low-poly-game-characters-vwnumirfcea , https://vsquad.art/blog/modeling-guide-to-achieving-good-face-topology)
- Quads where things bend; tris OK elsewhere; "every edge has a reason".
- Elbow/knee: three loops outside the bend, two inside; extra loop at shoulders/hips/neck; **density 3-4x higher on face and hands than thigh**.
- Face: loops around eye and mouth, a brow loop; eyes as sockets (at Synty density spheres or a painted face are accepted).
- No long thin tris; no evenly spaced rings ("same spacing everywhere reads as generated"); silhouette first from every angle.
- The six "accidental instead of authored" errors: uniform edge spacing, no plane breaks, smooth shading on a faceted mesh, one colour per model, perfect symmetry, floating/intersecting parts (https://sundaysundae.co/how-to-make-low-poly-look-good/ , https://www.designyourway.net/blog/low-poly-art/).

## 2. AI / LLM 3D generation 2023-2026

### 2a. LLM writes modelling code (anyCreature's family)
- 3D-GPT (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.12945), SceneCraft (ICML'24, https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01248), BlenderAlchemy: LLM -> Blender Python, scene graph first, VLM edits from renders. Works for scenes/props from primitives; not characters.
- **LL3M** (2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08228 , https://threedle.github.io/ll3m/): planner/retrieval(1 729 Blender-doc files)/coder/VLM-critic/verifier/user agents, Blender 4.4; convincing instruments, vehicles, furniture, trees. Limitation: "VLMs may still struggle to identify spatial artifacts ... disconnected handles or misaligned components require user-guided corrections."
- **ShapeCraft** (NeurIPS'25, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17603): graph of parts, each a code snippet, refined node by node with an evaluator; beats flat code agents. **Procedura** (2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26238): parts joined by **typed, machine-checked mates**, placement solved from mated frames "rather than guessing it", one diagnosed fix per critic round; best judged quality of any code agent, sharpest edges, editable output. aDSL (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.17975) co-designs the DSL with the agent.
- **3DCodeBench** (NeurIPS'26, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01057 , 12 VLMs, 212 classes, Blender 5.0): "failures mostly arise from API mismatches, while successful renders still suffer from disconnected or floating components"; more thinking + multi-turn refinement help; the environment must give "high-fidelity feedback".
- BlenderLLM (https://github.com/FreedomIntelligence/BlenderLLM), MeshCoder (https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14879), CAD-Recode (https://github.com/filaPro/cad-recode): fine-tuned code emitters; general finding (LLM-Primitives, SIGGRAPH Asia'25, https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3757377.3763857): "CSG captures mechanical objects precisely, organic freeform surfaces are expressed less naturally".
- Takeaway: **code agents win when the DSL has typed parts with checked joins and the loop has a real renderer + critic.** Their weakness (organic surfaces) is exactly where a box-model + flat-shade idiom helps: "organic" becomes a handful of ring scales, not a surface to describe.

### 2b. Mesh-as-tokens
MeshGPT -> MeshAnything V2 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02555 , ~1.6 k faces, shape-conditioned) -> BPT/DeepMesh/MeshRipple (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.07514) -> **QuadGPT** (ICLR'26, https://hitcslj.github.io/QuadGPT/), Meshtron, QuadLink (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.16813) quad-dominant artist meshes; MeshFIM (https://arxiv.org/html/2605.08744) local low-poly editing. LLaMA-Mesh (https://github.com/nv-tlabs/LLaMA-Mesh) tops out at 500 faces, coarse coordinates. All need a GPU and a shape condition; they are a retopology replacement, not a design tool. Not runnable here (4 CPU, no GPU).

### 2c. Image/text -> 3D
Hunyuan3D 2.1 (open, PBR, 10-29 GB VRAM, https://github.com/tencent-hunyuan/hunyuan3d-2.1), TRELLIS, Tripo 3.x, Meshy 6/7 (quad + T-pose, auto-rig, Smart Topology 1 k-300 k, https://www.meshy.ai/tutorials/make-low-poly-3d-models), Rodin. Reviews (https://medium.com/illumination/meshy-ai-3d-generator-review-2026-full-production-pipeline-tested-298b006b4a42 , https://www.3daistudio.com/blog/hitem3d-vs-meshy-vs-tripo-comparison): "props production-ready; hero characters need a human pass; removes the tedious 80 %, not the artistic 20 %"; rig weights over-smoothed at shoulders, biped-only in most tools. Output is a dense scan-like surface; "low poly" export = decimation, not authored facets. GPU or paid API. Useful as a *reference-sheet* generator, not as the modeller.

### 2d. Auto-rig / retopo
UniRig (SIGGRAPH'25, MIT, https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/UniRig ; bipeds, quadrupeds, birds, insects; GPU), MagicArticulate (https://chaoyuesong.github.io/MagicArticulate/), Tripo rig v2.5 (quadruped/hexapod/serpentine, paid), AccuRig 2.0 (free, biped), Mixamo (biped) - survey https://app.cinevva.com/guides/free-character-animations-rigging. For a mesh authored *with a skeleton in hand* none is needed: Blender armature + automatic heat weights worked first time on my proof. Retopo: Instant Meshes (no pip wheel), **QuadriFlow is inside bpy** (`bpy.ops.object.quadriflow_remesh`), Quad Remesher commercial best (https://superrendersfarm.com/article/quad-remesher-blender-retopology , https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/meshes/retopology.html). Only relevant if a sculpt/SDF surface is the source.

### 2e. VLM-as-judge reliability (relevant to the blind readers)
3D-DefectBench (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10826): humans agree on *geometry* defects (kappa 0.79-0.97) far more than texture; VLMs track that. Cross-Model VLM-Judge (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18451): fixed 24-view rig, two judge families, **mandatory position-bias correction (ask both orders, keep only consistent verdicts)**; judge agreement kappa 0.66; "render-CLIP is at chance". Judging-to-Improve (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20364); VLM3D split Content-Match / Geometric-Quality (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.14271). VibeWorlding (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.15265) and Snap (https://eng.snap.com/spatial_intelligence): agents "achieve the correct displacement magnitude but move the object in the opposite direction"; handedness, reversed cross products, wrong depth. CoT degrades visual-spatial reasoning (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.16060). LLM 3D self-correction from renders is limited (https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.07010).

## 3. LLM strengths / weaknesses and the division of labour
Good: part list with named joins; sequential modelling programs (extrude, place ring, loop cut) - my 200-line bmesh script ran first time; material/colour plan; skeleton placed at the rings it made (no auto-rig guesswork); naming a *category* of defect from a render.
Bad: absolute numbers blind (20-40 % off; pass 2 after one look was far closer); handedness/depth from one view; describing smooth organic surfaces analytically (the loft trap); self-grading; many small numeric nudges (each costs a full context re-read; literature converges on "one diagnosed fix per round, few rounds").
Division: LLM = brief, part graph, skeleton, modelling program, materials, anim keys, fix list. Deterministic = mirror/clip, flat/auto-smooth, bevel, triangulate, manifold, auto-weights, glTF, orbit metrics, contract, 48 px thumbs. VLM (context-free, two-order de-biased) = "what is this?" at 48 px, "which is bolder", geometry-defect checklist on a fixed rig. Human = one direction question, final look.

## 4. Critique of the current cards/gates/readers
KEEP: order -> one question -> brief slots; "discard first three ideas"/"one exaggeration"; context-free reads at 48 px with canary + shuffled labels and rank scoring; the 8+2 orbit (`outline.py` accepted my Blender GLB unchanged - the harness is engine-agnostic); "same symptom twice -> restart"; "author everything, build once"; state on disk; one modelling agent; the output contract (`glbcheck.mjs` also passed the Blender GLB).
CHANGE: (1) **the representation** - replace chains+lofts+bolted parts with skeleton + a **box-modelling program** whose verbs are the human verbs (`ring`, `extrude_face`+`place_quad`, `loop_cut`, `bevel`, `mirror`, `asymmetry`, `material(faces)`); one mesh, limbs extruded from the torso, grid caps, no buried root rings; this deletes most of `checks.js` (root_containment, part_attachment, junction skin/normal blend, seam-safe colour, mirror_distortion) because the seam no longer exists. (2) **style target** - flat-shaded low poly 500-3 000 tris by default, auto-smooth optional, instead of smooth loft + AO. (3) **readers** - keep identity read but ask each batch in two orders/shuffles and keep only consistent verdicts; add one fixed-rig geometry-defect checklist read at MID (floating/fused/missing/pose/extra); and **let the modeller SEE a render after every build and write a fix list** - cards forbid this, opposite to LL3M/Procedura/3DCodeBench and every human tutorial; self-*grading* stays banned, self-*looking* must be allowed. (4) **reference sheet** - a numeric front/side/top brief (shoulder height, body length/depth, head length, knee position) before geometry. (5) MID rounds = one diagnosed fix each (Procedura); LOW keeps three variants. (6) rewrite STYLE.md in loop/ring terms (it is written against taper/bias/exp).
DROP: `soft_mass` (measures a loft symptom); the junction/AO/L1-L8 stack as a requirement; the "nothing may launch a renderer" rule (Workbench renders in 0.4 s on CPU); `example_copy`, `mirror_distortion`, `part_attachment`, `root_containment`.

## 5. Ranked recommendations
1. **LLM box-models in Blender (`bpy`): skeleton-aware extrude/loop-cut program, mirror+clip, flat shade, material slots or vertex colour, armature + auto-weights, glTF; 2-3 render-critique rounds; keep the harness's orbit/identity/contract gates.** Synty/Quaternius-grade, "made by a person". Cost: 300 MB wheel + 3 apt libs; proof in 1 h. Risk: proportions need the feedback loop + reference sheet; hands/faces crude at 500 tris (fine for the style). **Do this.**
2. Same, but port the box-model verbs into the zero-dep Node engine (1.5-3 k lines re-implementing bmesh; no renderer; CC/bevel/auto-weights/quadriflow extra). Only if zero-dependency is a hard product rule. The previous review's cage->CC->shrink-wrap plan is a variant that keeps the loft as SDF source: it removes the tube read but not the "smooth anonymous blob" read - it still needs a sculpt layer and eye/mouth loops, i.e. the rest of a modeller.
3. Text -> image reference sheet -> (1). Better proportions and designs; needs an image API/GPU. Add later as the reference stage.
4. Text/image -> 3D (Hunyuan3D/Meshy/Tripo) -> remesh -> UniRig. Dense scan look, decimated not authored, soft weights, GPU/paid, no edit handle. Not this product's look; reference source only.
5. Mesh-as-tokens (QuadGPT/MeshAnything) on a shape from 4. Artist-like quads but research code, GPU, <= ~2 k faces, no part/style control. Watch.
6. SDF + surface nets. Seam-free but voxel-crumpled. No.
First experiment (next): three shipped orders (wolf, giant, raven-wyvern) as bpy programs with a numeric reference first, two critique rounds, auto-weights, three clips, GLB -> existing `outline.py`/`judge.mjs`/`glbcheck.mjs` + blind identity read -> owner and two fresh readers: "which was made by a person?". If it wins: `engine/` -> `tools/build.py` (bpy), `SYNTAX.md` -> verb sheet, `checks.js` -> contract + manifold + attack-reach + clip integrity, STYLE.md rewritten.

## 6. Proof (run) - honest judgement
Script `$S/wolf_boxmodel.py` (~200 lines, human steps in the header). `$S/proof_wolf.glb`: 268 verts / 308 faces / 580 tris, 25 bones, `idle` clip, vertex colours + materials. `glbcheck.mjs`: OK. `outline.py`: "orbit holds: no azimuth collapses against its neighbours, the head reads" (head 31 % az000, 19-20 % obliques). Renders (Cycles CPU): `proof_wolf_beauty.png` (same 3/4 as `example/wolf_beauty.png`), `_side`, `_front`, `_top`, `_back34`, `_posed_f24` (skinned mid-clip), `_clay`, `_workbench`, `proof_wolf_wire_beauty.png` / `_wire_side.png` (topology: rings only at rump/hip/waist/chest/shoulder/neck/skull/brow/muzzle/nose, legs extruded from the torso, quad loops at the leg roots), `pass1/` (before critique), `compare_engine_vs_boxmodel.png` (engine | pass 1 | pass 2), `orbit_proof/orbit_sheet.png`. 81 s per pass incl. five Cycles renders; model+rig+export ~3 s.
- It reads as a wolf/dog at a glance and as a low-poly asset a person made: flat facets, chunky masses, one continuous surface, legs part of the body, saddle/belly/paw colour plan, nose + yellow eyes, tilted ear, swaying tail. Next to the engine wolf (more stuff: tufts, claws, AO, gradient) the engine still looks like inflated tubes with a head plugged into a neck; the box model has less and looks designed.
- Not yet Synty quality: thin/jagged hind legs (needs one more loop, wider thigh), tail rooted low, no mouth line, sphere eyes, no claws, no weight shift in the bind pose, 1 cm jitter not yet reading as intent. One more round fixes the first three; mouth line + eye sockets need two loops on the head. "Credible first pass by a junior artist" - about where the pipeline should be *before* the gates.
- The feedback loop mattered more than the first numbers: pass 2 changed ten numbers after one look and is visibly better.
- Not needed at all in the proof: root containment, part attachment, junction blends, mirror distortion, soft-mass, faceted-body block, AO bake, L1-L8. Mirror-clip, flat shading and automatic weights replaced all of them.
