Units: L = body length nose-tip to tail-tip = 1.0. Built at L = 2.00 m (spec metres = L x 2.0).

## The one exaggeration

The snout-to-tail LENGTH: a flat, very long, very low silhouette — length:height about 6:1 —
everything else (sprawl, tiny legs, scute row shrinking to a point) serves that horizontal line.
