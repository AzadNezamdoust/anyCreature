# Zoo review round 2 (Opus, blind, orbit18 + side_by_side)

Part names below are by role; map to the matching part in each `<creature>.py`.

## fox: 7
1. Front view torso and chest too narrow (stick-like at az0) compared with the ref's full chest. Fix: chest/torso part: widen X by ~25% at the shoulder ring, and taper it toward the waist.
2. The tail is short and droops to the ground, so it reads as a limp brush. Ref tail is thick and lifted. Fix: tail chain: make it about 30% longer, double the mid-section radius and raise the base pitch so the tail arcs up and back.
3. The chest bib is a shaggy, jagged white sheet, which breaks the crisp-plane style. Fix: bib/ruff part: cut the jag count to 3-4 large planes.

## bear: 6
1. At az0 the body is far too narrow; the ref is a wide, round mass. Fix: torso and shoulder rings: widen X by ~35%, and push the legs outward.
2. The rear is a flat cut-off box, and the shoulder hump is missing. Fix: rump: add a rounded rear ring. Torso: raise the top vertex over the shoulders by ~8%.
3. The head is small and the muzzle cone is pointed; the ref has a broad, rounded head. Fix: head: widen the cranium ~20% and blunt the muzzle tip ring.

## boar: 7
1. The snout is a long pale cone with a pink disc. Fix: snout: shorten by ~15% and drop the pale tone to body grey; keep a small pink nose disc only.
2. The legs are thin and straight for the bulk. Fix: leg parts: thicken the upper legs ~25%.
3. The dorsal mane is a row of small spikes that is lost at az0 and az180. Fix: mane: raise the ridge height ~1.5x and run it onto the nape.

## tiger: 6
1. The head is boxy, with a flat face and no clear muzzle; at az0 the face reads as a mask. Fix: head: add a short muzzle block forward and narrow the forehead planes.
2. The white cheek ruff is split into jagged flaps (az45 and az315). Fix: ruff: reduce it to 2 planes per side.
3. The body is too short and tall, so it reads more as a dog. Fix: torso: lengthen Z ~15% and lower the back ~5%.

## stag: 7
1. The antlers are dense and spiky, a thicket at az0 and az180. Fix: antler: cut the tines to 4-5 per side and thicken the main beam.
2. The torso is a straight tube with a flat back and belly. Fix: torso: add a chest drop and a rump rise to the rings.
3. The rump patch has a hard rectangle edge at az90. Fix: rump patch: bevel it to follow the rump ring.

## snake: 5
1. It is almost flat on the ground, so it disappears at az0 and az180 (a thin sliver). Fix: body path: add vertical S-curves, or a raised head and neck at ~25% of the length.
2. The head is a small flat box that is barely wider than the neck. Fix: head: make it ~1.6x the neck width, as a wedge.
3. The pattern blocks are square tiles. Fix: pattern: use diamond saddles.

## crocodile: 7
1. It is very low and thin, and at az0 and az180 it reads as just legs. Fix: torso: raise the body height ~20% and widen the belly.
2. The legs are tiny and short. Fix: legs: scale ~1.3x and splay the elbows outward.
3. The scute rows are many small even bumps (noisy). Fix: scutes: use fewer, bigger ridges, in 2 rows.

## shark: 6
1. **Broken pose:** the pectoral and pelvic fins act as legs touching the ground. Fix: pectoral fins: rotate them up to about 30 deg below horizontal and set the root height so nothing touches the floor; lift the shark off the ground plane.
2. The gill slits are small black dashes, which is fussy. Fix: gills: replace them with 3-5 flat stripe planes, or drop them.
3. The belly line is boxy and stepped. Fix: belly ring: smooth it to a single convex curve.

## koi: 5
1. **Broken pose:** the pectoral and pelvic fins splay to the floor like legs, so it reads as a walking fish. Fix: pectorals: rotate them to near-horizontal, swept back; lift the fish off the ground.
2. The body is a fat block with a stepped back. Fix: torso: use smoother rings and taper the peduncle.
3. The dorsal fin is a thin spike line. Fix: dorsal: use a long, low sail running about 50% of the back.

## chameleon: 7
1. The casque is small and the head is boxy. Fix: casque: raise it into a taller helmet ridge.
2. The colour stripes dominate over the shape. Fix: palette: reduce the stripe contrast.
3. The legs are straight pillars; a chameleon needs bent zygodactyl legs. Fix: legs: add an elbow and knee bend and use a mitten foot.

## dragon: 6
1. The wings are small and folded tight, so it reads as a lizard from the front and rear. Fix: wing bones: scale them ~1.4x and open the membrane wider.
2. The neck is short and the head is small. Fix: neck: lengthen ~25%.
3. The tail is very long and thin, with lots of small spikes. Fix: tail: shorten ~15% and use fewer, bigger spikes.

## goblin: 7
1. The ears are huge flat triangles that dominate every view. Fix: ears: scale ~0.75 and add a slight droop.
2. The nose is a long beak, so the side view is odd. Fix: nose: shorten ~30%.
3. The legs are thick and the feet large. Fix: limbs: thin them slightly.

## golem: 6
1. The head is tiny and sunk between the shoulders, so it is lost at az90 and in the rear views. Fix: head: scale ~1.4x and raise it.
2. The side view is a thin slab, and the arms hide the body. Fix: torso: deepen Y ~30%.
3. The face plates and knuckle chips are fussy. Fix: chest and knuckle plates: merge them.

## ogre: 6
1. The head is sunk into the torso, with no neck; the profile reads as a hunchback lump. Fix: head: raise it ~10% and pull it back over the spine.
2. The belly is a flat cream panel. Fix: belly: give it a forward bulge ring.
3. The arms are straight tubes. Fix: arms: add a forearm taper and a bigger fist.

## knight: 7
1. The helmet is small for the bulky pauldrons. Fix: helm: scale ~1.2x.
2. The cape's back emblem is a big ring decal, which is busy. Fix: emblem: simplify it to a flat disc, or remove it.
3. The legs are short and the stance stumpy. Fix: legs: lengthen ~10%.

## octopus: 7
1. The arms are thin and uniform. Fix: arms: increase the root width ~1.5x and taper to the tips.
2. The mantle tilts back too far (az90). Fix: mantle: reduce the pitch ~15 deg.
3. The eyes are small dots on the side. Fix: eyes: raise them onto small bumps.

## spider: 8
1. The cephalothorax is small, low and hidden by the legs. Fix: cephalothorax: scale ~1.2x and raise it.
2. The leg stripes are dense and noisy. Fix: legs: use one band per segment.
3. The abdomen is a clean ball; the ref may want a slightly egg-shaped one. Fix: abdomen: lengthen Z ~10%.

## crab: 8
1. The claws are held very high above the body, and the az90 silhouette is odd. Fix: claw arms: lower the shoulder pitch ~20 deg.
2. The legs are uniform thick tubes. Fix: legs: taper them to points.
3. The carapace is a smooth dome; it needs a front edge. Fix: carapace: add a flat front rim.

## owl: 5
1. **Face rule violation:** big orange-ring cartoon eyes inside a pale face mask. Fix: eyes: shrink them to small round lenses and drop the orange rings. Facial disc: tone it to the body colour.
2. The body is a tall block, and the wing plates at az90 and az135 are large dark triangles. Fix: wings: fold them flatter to the body and reduce the contrast.
3. The legs are long, with visible shanks. Fix: legs: shorten them and hide them in a belly skirt.

## raven_wyvern: 4
1. **Species does not read:** it is a plain raven, with no wyvern features (no wing-arms, no tail spade, no horns). Fix: add membrane wings or wing-arms, a spined tail tip and small horns.
2. The tail is an extremely long, thin stick (az90). Fix: tail: shorten ~35% and fan it.
3. It is near-black overall, so the shapes are lost. Fix: palette: lift the values and keep a blue accent.
