## fox: 8
1. Tail reads as a thin wedge with a frayed white tip; in the sheet it is a bushy brush. Fix: `tail`: raise mid-section radius ~1.5x and give the tip 3-4 broad flat cards, not many slivers.
2. The white chest bib is built from many small spiky shards that break the flat-plane style. Fix: `chest_ruff`: merge it into 2-3 large planes.
3. Legs are slightly long, so the body floats. Fix: `leg_*`: shorten 8-10 % and drop the torso to match.

## bear: 8
1. The shoulder hump is weak in side view; the back is nearly flat. Fix: `torso`: raise the shoulder ring ~10 % above the hip ring.
2. Front view: the head sinks into the chest and there is no neck break. Fix: `head`: move it forward 5 % and lift the muzzle plane.
3. Claws are tiny pale slivers that read as noise. Fix: `claw_*`: use fewer, thicker claws (4 per paw), length +30 %.

## boar: 7
1. The dorsal crest is a row of separate floating blade cards that look stuck on. Fix: `mane/crest`: one continuous saw-tooth strip rooted into the spine.
2. The tusks are thin and the cheek plates flare outward like a face mask. Fix: `cheek_*`: flatten them into the head; `tusk_*`: thicker, curved up 20 deg.
3. Legs are too thin and long for a boar mass. Fix: `leg_*`: radius +20 %, length -10 %.

## tiger: 6
1. Side view: the head is small and wedge-like and the neck is long; it reads as a dog or wolf, not a big cat. Fix: `head`: widen it 25 %, shorten the snout, add round cheek ruff planes.
2. The ears are pointed white triangles; the sheet has round dark ears. Fix: `ear_*`: make them rounded, dark backs, smaller.
3. The torso is boxy, with no chest depth or paw mass. Fix: `torso`: deepen the chest 15 %; `paw_*`: enlarge 30 %.

## stag: 7
1. The antler tines are thin sticks, and some read disconnected at a distance. Fix: `antler_*`: thicken the main beam and root every tine into it.
2. The neck mane is a dark shard cluster that makes the chest muddy. Fix: `neck_mane`: 3-4 large planes.
3. The legs are spindly with no hock angle. Fix: `hind_leg`: add the reverse hock bend.

## snake: 6
1. The body is a near-straight noodle with a single bend; it lacks coil/S-curve mass. Fix: `spine` path: add 2 lateral S-bends, raise the head 20 % off the ground.
2. The head is barely wider than the neck. Fix: `head`: widen the jaw 30 %, give it a flat top plane.
3. The tail taper is too abrupt. Fix: `tail`: taper over the last 30 %.

## crocodile: 6
1. The legs are too long and upright; a croc sprawls. Fix: `leg_*`: splay them out 30 deg, lower the body to near the ground.
2. The snout is short for the head. Fix: `snout`: lengthen it 25 %, flatten it.
3. The scute row is dense, tiny bumps. Fix: `scutes`: fewer, larger, in 2 rows.

## shark: 7
1. The pectoral fins are thin and droop. Fix: `pec_fin_*`: broaden the chord 30 %, angle 15 deg down only.
2. The caudal fin lobes are thin; the upper lobe is too long. Fix: `tail_fin`: thicken it, lower-lobe ratio 0.7.
3. The gill slits are black stripes and read like decals. Fix: `gills`: make them shallow inset planes.

## koi: 5
1. **Broken:** the pelvic and anal fins hang below the belly like legs, so the fish "stands" (front/rear views show four stilts). Fix: `pelvic_fin_*`, `anal_fin`: rotate them back about 60 deg and tuck them against the belly.
2. The body is too slender, with a flat top; the sheet koi is plump and round. Fix: `body`: raise the girth radius 30 % mid-body and round the cross-section.
3. The barbels are long white tusks pointing down. Fix: `barbel_*`: halve the length, thinner, angled forward.

## chameleon: 7
1. The casque is a tall yellow cone that reads as a hat. Fix: `casque`: lower it 30 % and blend it back into the neck.
2. The eye has no turret cone; it is a flat dot. Fix: `eye`: add a small cone turret with the lens at its tip (keep the lens small).
3. The feet are flat pale plates, not zygodactyl grips. Fix: `foot_*`: split each into 2 opposed mitten blocks.

## dragon: 6
1. The neck and head are short and squat; side view lacks the S-neck of the sheet. Fix: `neck`: lengthen it 40 % and lift the head.
2. The wings are folded low and flat; front and rear views lack the tall wing silhouette. Fix: `wing_*`: raise the shoulder joint and open them 25 deg upward.
3. The legs are thin and the body boxy, with no chest/belly bulk. Fix: `torso`: deepen the chest and belly plates; `leg_*`: thicken the thighs 30 %.

## goblin: 4
1. **Broken:** the ears are huge two-tone horizontal spikes (salmon plus dark-green tips), each roughly the length of the head ×1.5. They dominate every orbit view, and the top view looks like a dart. Fix: `ear_*`: scale the length to 0.6x, single skin colour, tilt 25 deg up and back.
2. The head/nose is a long downward hook, and the face reads as an elephant trunk at +45 az0. Fix: `nose`: halve the length and point it forward.
3. The arms hang to the knees with no hands; the feet are flat slabs. Fix: `hand_*`: add 3-finger mitts; `foot_*`: shorten 25 %.

## golem: 6
1. The shoulder boulders rise above the head and the head is tiny; front view reads as a hunched gorilla. Fix: `head`: scale 1.4x and lift it above the shoulder line; `shoulder_rock_*`: lower them 15 %.
2. The face is a visor slit with glowing eyes (a mask). Fix: `face`: plain planes, 2 small round lens eyes.
3. The blue chest rune is a thin outline and reads as a liner. Fix: `rune`: one solid inset glyph plane, or remove it.

## ogre: 7
1. The head is small and sunk into the hunched shoulders. Fix: `head`: scale 1.2x and bring it forward.
2. The arms are thick and even with no forearm taper; the hands are blobs. Fix: `forearm/hand`: taper the wrist, add fingers.
3. Belly and strap are fine, but the loincloth is bulky. Fix: `loincloth`: thin it 30 %.

## knight: 6
1. The helmet is a featureless capsule with a dark slot; the head reads as a bucket. Fix: `helm`: add a visor ridge and a crest plane.
2. The limbs are stiff tubes with no pauldron/knee-cop mass, so the silhouette is generic. Fix: `pauldron_*`, `knee_*`: add flared plates.
3. The sword floats at the hip without a grip. Fix: `sword`: attach it to a belt scabbard.

## octopus: 5
1. The mantle stands vertical like a tall cylinder on a base; the sheet mantle is egg-shaped and tilted back 45 deg. Fix: `mantle`: tilt it back 40 deg and widen it 20 %.
2. The arms are thick, short and uneven; some point up (side view) and they do not taper to whips. Fix: `arm_*`: taper them to 10 % radius at the tip, length +30 %, all resting in a low radial star.
3. One eye is buried in a mantle crease; the eyes are not bulging. Fix: `eye_*`: small turret bumps either side of the head.

## spider: 5
1. **Broken:** the front view shows two downward fang blocks on a face plate like a snout; the head reads as a dog. Fix: `chelicera_*`: shorten them 50 %, tuck them under; flatten the `cephalothorax` front.
2. The abdomen is a flat disc with an orange outline ring (rear view), not a sphere. Fix: `abdomen`: round the cross-section (depth equal to width) and use band stripes only.
3. The legs splay as straight sticks with no knee rise above the body. Fix: `leg_*`: raise the femur-patella knee above the carapace line.

## crab: 3
1. **Broken/jumbled claws:** the claws are large hollow fists with loose cream fingers fanned upward (the model top/front views look like hands); the right claw is swung up over the body in side/hero. Fix: `claw_*`: rebuild as a solid propodus block plus 2 fingers (fixed + dactyl) closing horizontally, both at mirrored poses in front.
2. The legs are long straight spikes that lift the body high; the sheet legs are short, jointed and curved. Fix: `leg_*`: shorten them 40 %, add a knee bend, lower the carapace.
3. The carapace is a tall dome with a cream underside band. Fix: `carapace`: flatten it 30 %, widen it into an oval.

## owl: 5
1. The body is tilted forward like a hawk, and the tail is a long wedge (side view); an owl is upright and compact. Fix: `torso`: rotate it to vertical, `tail`: shorten 60 %.
2. The ear tufts are huge cat ears; front view reads as a cat. Fix: `ear_tuft_*`: thin them to narrow feather points, 50 % size, angled outward.
3. The facial disc is weak; the chest has a shard ruff. Fix: `face_disc`: one flat round plane; `chest`: merge the shards.

## raven_wyvern: 6
1. The neck is long and thin and the head small; the profile reads as a crow/heron. The sheet raven has a heavy chest and shaggy throat. Fix: `neck`: shorten it 30 %; `chest`: deepen it 20 %; add throat hackle planes.
2. Front view: the wings are narrow slabs out from the body with a gap; the body is a thin pole. Fix: `wing_*`: fold them against the body, widen the shoulders.
3. The beak is fine. The rear tail is striped light bands. Fix: `tail`: one dark plane, wedge-shaped.
