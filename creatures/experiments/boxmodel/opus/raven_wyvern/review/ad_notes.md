# Raven-wyvern: REBUILD brief (art direction round 1, both reviewers: REBUILD, score 4)

Both art directors agree on the verdict: **it reads as a crow, parrot or
cockatoo, not a wyvern.** Every dragon cue is a small piece glued onto a bird
base. The fix is a new stage 1 with the wyvern silhouette IN THE BASE.

## Must be in the stage-1 base (identity)

- **Neck:** a visible S-neck about 0.4× body length (roughly one head long).
  Not a bird with no neck.
- **Tail:** a long tapering reptile tail, at least as long as the body, with
  the fan only at its tip.
- **Head:** ~15% smaller relative to the body than the current build.
- **Bill:** a raven bill (not a parrot's), twice as long as it is deep, with
  the hook only in the last quarter.
- **Wings as forelimbs:** the upper arm, forearm and one long finger are their
  own masses, folded along the flanks. The wrist is carried high and forward
  and ends in a thumb claw. The folded membrane reads as a bat/dragon wing.
- **Brow:** a stage-2 plane in the base over the eye. The last build stabbed a
  brow piece through the skull.

## Stage 3 pieces: thick, never paper

- **Membrane:** a solid folded piece, ≥ 3 cm thick. The spars are tubes
  ≥ 4 cm in section, seated into the membrane with no coplanar faces and no
  needle slivers.
- **Horns:** 2 main swept-back horn cones (5–6-sided section, base ≥ 30% of
  the length) and 2 smaller ones, in dark horn or bone. No cockatoo fan.
- **Dorsal spikes:** 7–9 graduated wedges, 2–3× the old height at the
  shoulders, tapering toward the tail and swept back ~30°. No two neighbours
  the same size.
- **Hackles:** 5–7 overlapping feather clumps of varied length, each ≥ 3× the
  old blade thickness.
- **Eye:** a disc 1.5× the old size, with a dark pupil, under the brow plane.
- **Tail fan:** 3–5 thick vanes of varied length and angle.

## Colour

- Body black-blue, dark but with facets that read.
- The bill is dark horn (~20% value) with a bone-coloured tip, **not orange**:
  the orange bill was the main parrot cue.
- Membrane and fan purple, eye amber.

## Clips

- **Attack:** a wind-up with the head drawn back and the wings half-spread
  (shoulder abduction 40–60°), then the lunge. A wyvern threat display, not a
  bird peck.
- **Move:** a visible stride.
- **Rig:** use `armature(..., roll='auto')` for this new build.

## Keep from the old build

- The folded membrane wings with radiating spar lines are the right cue.
- The amber eye under a hard dark brow gives a hostile face.
- The bird legs with 3+1 black talons, planted flat.

## Gates

- Every kit gate, including tech QA.
- The blind identity read (`judge/raven_wyvern.brief.md`): a stranger must
  say wyvern, dragon or drake.
