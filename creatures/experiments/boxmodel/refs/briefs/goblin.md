# goblin: design brief (identical for settings N, G and O)

- **Identity:** reads as a goblin. Accepted words: imp, gremlin, hobgoblin, kobold.
- **Size and pose:** height 1.1 m, length 0.6 m, width 0.8 m across the ears. Standing crouched, arms hanging. It faces -Y, the left flank is +X, and the feet stand on z = 0.
- **Silhouette cue (it must be in the stage-1 base):** small hunched humanoid with a big head, long horizontal pointed ears, hooked nose, arms to the knees.
- **Head and face:** big domed head, heavy brow, yellow eyes, long hooked nose, wide fanged grin, long horizontal ears.
- **Body and limbs:** thin limbs, pot belly over a belt, clawed three-fingered hands, three-toed feet, crouched stance.
- **Detail pieces (stage 3):** belt with a chunky buckle, tattered loincloth, claws, fangs.
- **Palette (4-8 flat colours):**
  - green skin: `#6aa84f`
  - light green belly: `#9ccc7a`
  - dark brown belt: `#4a3222`
  - brown cloth: `#7a5a3a`
  - cream fang: `#efe6cf`
  - yellow eye: `#f2d23c`
- **Clips:** idle: fidget and look around; move: sneaky crouched walk; attack: claw swipe.
- **Budget:** 500-3000 triangles in total; stage 1 has 400-1500.
- **Style:** a stylised low-poly game creature of the Quaternius or Synty kind. Big readable masses, clear planes, flat colours, and close-ups that hold up.
