# bear: design brief (identical for settings N, G and O)

- **Identity:** reads as a bear. Accepted words: grizzly, brown bear, black bear.
- **Size and pose:** height 1.0 m at the hump, length 1.7 m, width 0.7 m. Standing on all four legs, head low. It faces -Y, the left flank is +X, and the feet stand on z = 0.
- **Silhouette cue (it must be in the stage-1 base):** big shoulder hump as the highest point, head carried low, heavy body, thick legs.
- **Head and face:** broad dished face, pale muzzle block with a black nose pad, small dark eyes, round ears.
- **Body and limbs:** shoulder hump, deep body, thick legs tapering to the wrist, plantigrade paws with dark claws, stub tail.
- **Detail pieces (stage 3):** claws, darker lower legs, lighter chest plane.
- **Palette (4-8 flat colours):**
  - brown fur: `#6b4a33`
  - dark fur: `#4a3222`
  - pale muzzle: `#c9a57d`
  - black nose: `#1e1714`
  - dark claw: `#2a221e`
  - eye: `#151010`
- **Clips:** idle: breathing and sniffing; move: heavy walk; attack: lunge with a paw swipe.
- **Budget:** 500-3000 triangles in total; stage 1 has 400-1500.
- **Style:** a stylised low-poly game creature of the Quaternius or Synty kind. Big readable masses, clear planes, flat colours, and close-ups that hold up.
