# stag: design brief (identical for settings N, G and O)

- **Identity:** reads as a stag. Accepted words: deer, elk, red deer, moose, reindeer, caribou, buck.
- **Size and pose:** height 1.7 m to the antler tips, length 1.8 m, width 0.6 m. Standing on all four legs, head up. It faces -Y, the left flank is +X, and the feet stand on z = 0.
- **Silhouette cue (it must be in the stage-1 base):** tall branching antlers, upright neck with a dark mane, deep chest, slender legs.
- **Head and face:** long tapered head, black nose, pale jaw line, dark eyes, leaf-shaped ears, antlers with brow tines pointing forward and crowned tips.
- **Body and limbs:** deep chest, slender legs with knees and hocks, small black hooves, cream rump patch, short hanging tail.
- **Detail pieces (stage 3):** antlers, neck mane with a V bib, tail.
- **Palette (4-8 flat colours):**
  - red-brown body: `#8a5a3a`
  - dark mane: `#6a4630`
  - pale cream: `#d9c3a0`
  - antler: `#d8c8a8`
  - black hoof: `#231a16`
  - eye: `#111111`
- **Clips:** idle: grazing and ear flick; move: walk; attack: head-down antler charge.
- **Budget:** 500-3000 triangles in total; stage 1 has 400-1500.
- **Style:** a stylised low-poly game creature of the Quaternius or Synty kind. Big readable masses, clear planes, flat colours, and close-ups that hold up.
