# giant: design brief (identical for settings N, G and O)

- **Identity:** reads as a giant. Accepted words: troll, ogre, titan.
- **Size and pose:** height 4.0 m, length 2.5 m, width 3.9 m across the arms. Standing hunched, arms hanging to the knees. It faces -Y, the left flank is +X, and the feet stand on z = 0.
- **Silhouette cue (it must be in the stage-1 base):** hunched mountain troll: small sunken head under massive shoulders, very long arms to the knees with huge fists, short thick legs.
- **Head and face:** small head set low, heavy brow, deep-set amber eyes, cream tusks rising from the lower jaw.
- **Body and limbs:** massive hump of shoulders and back, pot belly, short thick legs, big flat feet, fists with a knuckle row and separate fingers.
- **Detail pieces (stage 3):** moss clumps and a few flat rocks bedded on the shoulders, belt with a loincloth, stone-coloured nails.
- **Palette (4-8 flat colours):**
  - blue-grey hide: `#66727f`
  - dark hide: `#343b46`
  - stone: `#8b867c`
  - moss green: `#6f8f3a`
  - tan leather: `#a58c67`
  - cream tusk: `#d6c9a6`
  - amber eye: `#f4b427`
- **Clips:** idle: heavy breathing sway; move: lumbering walk; attack: two-handed overhead slam.
- **Budget:** 500-3000 triangles in total; stage 1 has 400-1500.
- **Style:** a stylised low-poly game creature of the Quaternius or Synty kind. Big readable masses, clear planes, flat colours, and close-ups that hold up.
