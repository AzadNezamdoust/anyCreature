# wolf: design brief (identical for settings N, G and O)

- **Identity:** reads as a wolf. Accepted words: dog, husky, coyote, canine, hound, jackal, dingo.
- **Size and pose:** height 1.1 m to the ear tips, length 1.7 m nose to tail tip, width 0.5 m. Standing on all four legs, head level. It faces -Y, the left flank is +X, and the feet stand on z = 0.
- **Silhouette cue (it must be in the stage-1 base):** wedge head with tall pointed ears, thick neck ruff, deep chest with a clear tuck-up, long legs, bushy brush tail with a dark tip carried low.
- **Head and face:** long wedge muzzle, black nose pad over a cream lip, amber eyes under a slight brow, tall pointed ears.
- **Body and limbs:** deep chest, narrow waist, long tapering legs, oval paws with the middle toes leading.
- **Detail pieces (stage 3):** neck ruff in clumps, dark back saddle, cream bib, tail brush with a dark tip, toe claws.
- **Palette (4-8 flat colours):**
  - grey fur: `#9c978e`
  - dark saddle: `#65636b`
  - tan legs: `#b59d76`
  - cream bib: `#efe6cf`
  - amber eye: `#e0a030`
  - black nose: `#1e1b1b`
- **Clips:** idle: breathing, ear twitch; move: trot in diagonal pairs; attack: head-down lunge and snap.
- **Budget:** 500-3000 triangles in total; stage 1 has 400-1500.
- **Style:** a stylised low-poly game creature of the Quaternius or Synty kind. Big readable masses, clear planes, flat colours, and close-ups that hold up.
