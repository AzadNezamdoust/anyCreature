# character: design brief (identical for settings N, G and O)

- **Identity:** reads as a cartoon character, a simple toon humanoid. Accepted words: human, person, man, boy, kid, child, humanoid, mannequin, doll, figure, toon.
- **Size and pose:** height 1.6 m to the top of the head, arm span 1.5 m in the T-pose, depth 0.3 m. Standing T-pose, arms straight out to the sides, palms down, feet slightly apart. It faces -Y, its left side is +X, and the feet stand on z = 0.
- **Silhouette cue (it must be in the stage-1 base):** a big round head about a quarter of the body height on a thin neck, a slim straight torso, long thin arms and legs.
- **Head and face:** a faceted sphere head; two big round cartoon eyes with white sclera, black pupils and a thin black outline; thin dark brows; a tiny pointed nose; a short straight line for the mouth.
- **Body and limbs:** slim torso with no clothing detail, thin arms with simple hands (thumb and a mitten of fingers slightly curled), thin straight legs, bare feet with toe blocks.
- **Detail pieces (stage 3):** eyes (sclera, pupil, outline), brows, mouth line.
- **Palette (4-8 flat colours):**
  - peach skin: `#f0b477`
  - eye white: `#f7f7f5`
  - black pupil and outline: `#151515`
  - dark brow and mouth: `#3a2f2a`
- **Clips:** idle: breathing and a blink; move: a relaxed walk with arm swing; attack: a punch.
- **Budget:** 500-3000 triangles in total; stage 1 has 400-1500.
- **Style:** a stylised low-poly game character. A faceted, gently rounded body, flat colours, and close-ups that hold up. The owner's concept image is the design target for the generated references.
