# frog: design brief (identical for settings N, G and O)

- **Identity:** reads as a frog. Accepted words: toad, bullfrog.
- **Size and pose:** height 0.3 m to the eye tops, length 0.5 m, width 0.8 m with the feet. Sitting, front legs straight, hind legs folded. It faces -Y, the left flank is +X, and the feet stand on z = 0.
- **Silhouette cue (it must be in the stage-1 base):** squat sitting frog tilted up at the front, big bulging eyes on top of a wide head, folded Z hind legs with long webbed feet.
- **Head and face:** wide flat head, big gold eyes with horizontal black pupils, wide black mouth line, cream jaw.
- **Body and limbs:** pear-shaped body, short front legs with splayed toes and pad tips, hind legs folded thigh-shin-foot with webbed feet.
- **Detail pieces (stage 3):** toe pads, darker irregular spots on the back, webbing.
- **Palette (4-8 flat colours):**
  - green skin: `#5fae3c`
  - dark green spots: `#2f6b2a`
  - cream belly: `#efe0b0`
  - gold eye: `#f2c230`
  - black pupil and mouth: `#151515`
- **Clips:** idle: throat pulse and blink; move: hop; attack: tongue lash.
- **Budget:** 500-3000 triangles in total; stage 1 has 400-1500.
- **Style:** a stylised low-poly game creature of the Quaternius or Synty kind. Big readable masses, clear planes, flat colours, and close-ups that hold up.
