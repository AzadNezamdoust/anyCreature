# raven-wyvern: design brief (identical for settings N, G and O)

- **Identity:** reads as a wyvern. Accepted words: dragon, drake, raven dragon, bird dragon, griffin, gryphon.
- **Size and pose:** height 1.4 m, length 2.3 m bill to tail tip, width 0.7 m with the wings folded. Standing on two legs, body pitched forward, wings folded. It faces -Y, the left flank is +X, and the feet stand on z = 0.
- **Silhouette cue (it must be in the stage-1 base):** a wyvern first: two legs, the wings ARE the forelimbs folded at rest, a long reptile tail longer than the body with a fin tip; raven traits second.
- **Head and face:** raven skull with a heavy black bill and a pale tip, amber eye, swept-back horns, shaggy throat hackles.
- **Body and limbs:** S-curved neck, deep keeled chest, forward-pitched crouch, bird legs with a reversed hock and talons, long tapering tail.
- **Detail pieces (stage 3):** folded membrane wings with tapered finger spars and a thumb claw, graded dorsal spines, tail fin, horns, hackles.
- **Palette (4-8 flat colours):**
  - blue-black plumage: `#2c3346`
  - purple membrane: `#6a3f9a`
  - black bill: `#1c1c22`
  - pale bill tip: `#d8cfb8`
  - amber eye: `#f0a020`
  - grey-brown shank: `#6e665e`
  - horn: `#e4dcc8`
- **Clips:** idle: breathing and head tilt; move: bird-like stalking walk; attack: neck lunge with the wings mantling.
- **Budget:** 500-3000 triangles in total; stage 1 has 400-1500.
- **Style:** a stylised low-poly game creature of the Quaternius or Synty kind. Big readable masses, clear planes, flat colours, and close-ups that hold up.
