# owl: design brief (identical for settings N, G and O)

- **Identity:** reads as a owl. Accepted words: barn owl, horned owl, eagle owl, owlet.
- **Size and pose:** height 0.64 m, length 0.41 m, width 0.35 m. Perched upright on its feet, wings folded. It faces -Y, the left flank is +X, and the feet stand on z = 0.
- **Silhouette cue (it must be in the stage-1 base):** upright egg-shaped body, rounded head with two ear tufts, big round eyes.
- **Head and face:** rounded head, two ear tufts, big orange eyes with black pupils, pale facial disc with a dark rim, V brows, small hooked beak.
- **Body and limbs:** upright body, folded wings along the sides, short fan tail, feathered legs with dark talons.
- **Detail pieces (stage 3):** ear tufts, brows, beak, talons, a lighter belly.
- **Palette (4-8 flat colours):**
  - brown feathers: `#7a5a3c`
  - dark brown: `#4a3424`
  - cream face and belly: `#efe3c6`
  - orange eye: `#f08a1c`
  - black pupil: `#111111`
  - beak and talon: `#d8b870`
- **Clips:** idle: head turn and blink; move: short hop with a wing flap; attack: talon strike with the wings spread.
- **Budget:** 500-3000 triangles in total; stage 1 has 400-1500.
- **Style:** a stylised low-poly game creature of the Quaternius or Synty kind. Big readable masses, clear planes, flat colours, and close-ups that hold up.
