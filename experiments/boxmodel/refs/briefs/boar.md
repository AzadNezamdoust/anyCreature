# boar: design brief (identical for settings N, G and O)

- **Identity:** reads as a boar. Accepted words: pig, hog, warthog, swine, peccary.
- **Size and pose:** height 0.9 m at the shoulder crest, length 1.4 m, width 0.46 m. Standing on all four legs, head low. It faces -Y, the left flank is +X, and the feet stand on z = 0.
- **Silhouette cue (it must be in the stage-1 base):** front-heavy body with a high shoulder hump, long wedge head ending in a flat snout disc, upward tusks, bristly crest along the spine.
- **Head and face:** long wedge head, flat round snout disc with two nostrils, small dark eye, pointed ears tilted forward, cream tusks curving up from the lower jaw.
- **Body and limbs:** high shoulder hump, body tapering to narrow hips, short legs with hock angles, cloven hooves, thin tail with a tuft.
- **Detail pieces (stage 3):** bristly dark crest from the nape to mid-back in blunt clumps, dark shoulder saddle, tusks.
- **Palette (4-8 flat colours):**
  - brown body: `#6f5040`
  - dark bristle: `#3e2c24`
  - pink-grey snout: `#b88e7e`
  - cream tusk: `#efe3c8`
  - black hoof: `#2a201c`
  - dark eye: `#1a1414`
- **Clips:** idle: sniffing and rooting; move: trot; attack: head-down charge with a tusk toss.
- **Budget:** 500-3000 triangles in total; stage 1 has 400-1500.
- **Style:** a stylised low-poly game creature of the Quaternius or Synty kind. Big readable masses, clear planes, flat colours, and close-ups that hold up.
