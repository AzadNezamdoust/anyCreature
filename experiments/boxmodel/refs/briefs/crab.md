# crab: design brief (identical for settings N, G and O)

- **Identity:** reads as a crab. Accepted words: hermit crab, spider crab, fiddler crab.
- **Size and pose:** height 0.4 m, length 1.2 m, width 1.5 m with the legs. Standing low, claws held in front. It faces -Y, the left flank is +X, and the feet stand on z = 0.
- **Silhouette cue (it must be in the stage-1 base):** wide low carapace with two big claws held in front and eight splayed walking legs with two bends each, body close to the ground.
- **Head and face:** eye stalks with bulb eyes on the front rim, a toothed front edge on the carapace.
- **Body and limbs:** broad carapace made of a few big planes, chunky claws with a real gap between the fingers, walking legs with a knee and a second bend, dark leg tips.
- **Detail pieces (stage 3):** claws, eye stalks, a small cluster of barnacles on the rear rim.
- **Palette (4-8 flat colours):**
  - red-orange shell: `#c8502e`
  - dark red ridge: `#8e3420`
  - cream belly: `#efc9a0`
  - dark tip: `#2a1c18`
  - black eye: `#111111`
  - barnacle: `#9a8474`
- **Clips:** idle: claw clicks; move: sideways scuttle; attack: claw snap.
- **Budget:** 500-3000 triangles in total; stage 1 has 400-1500.
- **Style:** a stylised low-poly game creature of the Quaternius or Synty kind. Big readable masses, clear planes, flat colours, and close-ups that hold up.
