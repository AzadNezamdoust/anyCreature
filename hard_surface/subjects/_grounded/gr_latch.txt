A fictional long handheld game weapon prop, a marksman-rifle shape in a grounded, lightly stylised look, olive green with black parts. A short black round muzzle tube with notched cuts at the very front. A long slim olive green forward shell with four oblong slots in a row along the left flank and one short slit nearest the front, with a short ridged black strip on top at its front. Above the body a flat black mount rail carrying a black round scope tube on two ring mounts: the scope has a wide round front end with a flat black lens and a thin gold band round it, a taller round knob on top and a smaller round knob on the left side. The olive body is flat topped, with one small green lamp window on its left flank above the grip and a small round dark button behind it. Below the body a straight black rectangular magazine, and an olive grip raked backward with a squared guard loop and a thin black trigger inside it. At the rear an olive stock with a raised cheek piece on top, a rectangular cutout in its lower part, and a tall flat black rear pad. The right flank is plain: no lamp, no button, no slots in the forward shell except the same four. About 1.05 m long, 0.08 m wide and 0.30 m tall.
