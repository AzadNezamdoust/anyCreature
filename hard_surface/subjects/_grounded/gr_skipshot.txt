A fictional compact handheld game weapon prop, a small sidearm shape in a grounded, lightly stylised look. A long slab-sided warm grey steel top shell with a flat top, three slanted grooves near its front end and five slanted grooves at its rear end on the side, a small black notched block on top at the rear and a small black blade on top at the front, and a round open black muzzle in its front face with a small round dark plug below it. Under the shell a matte black polymer body with a short flat plate under the muzzle end showing one thin groove, a squared guard loop with a thin curved black trigger inside it, and a thick grip raked backward with a rough-textured oval panel on its side and a flat black base plate closing the bottom of the grip. A small black lever and a small black button sit on the left flank above and behind the guard, and one small round amber lamp sits near the rear end of the grey shell on the left flank. The right flank is plain: the same grooves on the shell, a plain grip side, no lever, no button and no lamp. About 0.17 m long, 0.03 m wide and 0.12 m tall.
