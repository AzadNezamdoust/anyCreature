A fictional compact boxy handheld game weapon prop, a short carbine shape in a grounded, lightly stylised look. A deep olive green shell body runs from a chunky front block to a tall rear end with a thin black vertical pad at its rear edge. The flat top carries a long black ridged strip with a black notched sight block at its front end and another at its rear end, and a long black horizontal slot on the upper left flank. Two short rounded slots sit on the left side of the front block, above a ribbed black strip along its lower edge. At the front a short black round muzzle tube with a cage of four notched cuts on a black front block. Below the shell hangs a tall slanted closed loop handle in olive: its slim front leg and its thick rear leg with a rough-textured panel, with a thin curved black trigger inside the loop at the top. Behind the handle a tall black rectangular magazine with horizontal ribs hangs from the rear body. One small teal rectangular lamp window sits on the left flank above the magazine. The right flank is plain olive panel with no lamp and no slots on the front block. About 0.62 m long, 0.07 m wide and 0.27 m tall.
