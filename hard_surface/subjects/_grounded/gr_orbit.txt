A fictional compact two-hand sci-fi blaster, a stylised game prop built from simple shapes, slate-grey and dark charcoal. A wide, slab-sided slate-grey body with chamfered edges, as long as a forearm, forms the whole upper half: its front end is a broad flat rectangular block much wider than the rest of the body, with one wide horizontal slot open across its front face (the slot is dark inside, about three times as wide as tall) and a flat recessed rectangular panel on its top. On the left flank of the body, in the middle, sits one rectangular window with a rounded frame holding a single glowing blue lamp strip, with one small round screw head just behind it. At the rear top is a small angular notch-and-post sight and, beside it on the left flank, three short parallel vent slots; a thin pale bluish strip runs along the top edge of the left flank. Under the body, behind the window, hangs a rounded open trigger loop with one small curved lever inside it, and a thick dark charcoal grip raked backward with a textured side panel and a flared base. A second, shorter dark charcoal rectangular block with a ribbed side hangs straight down under the front part of the body, in front of the loop, with a flat base plate. The right flank is a plain mirror of the body shell with no lamp window, only the same vent slots and a small round button at the rear. About 0.34 m long, 0.10 m wide and 0.22 m tall.
