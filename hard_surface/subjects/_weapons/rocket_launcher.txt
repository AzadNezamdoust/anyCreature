A stylised shoulder-fired rocket launcher with a loaded rocket, one game weapon model. A long thick dark gunmetal launch tube, round in section, open at both ends, with a raised reinforcing ring at the front end and a wide flared cone-shaped blast venturi at the rear end. Out of the front of the tube sticks the loaded rocket: a fat matte black warhead shaped like a double cone, wider than the tube, with a short pointed nose fuze. Under the middle of the tube a matte black polymer pistol grip with a gunmetal trigger inside a rounded trigger guard, and in front of it a second matte black vertical fore grip. On top of the tube above the grips a boxy matte black optical sight on a short bracket with a flat dark blue lens facing the rear, and a small folding iron front sight post near the front ring. A matte black shoulder rest pad on the underside of the tube behind the grips. Safety-orange accents: a band round the rocket's warhead at its widest point, the tip of the nose fuze, and a band round the rim of the rear venturi. The sling swivels and the firing-mechanism cover are on the right flank, hidden in this view. About 1.30 m long, 0.16 m wide and 0.32 m tall.
