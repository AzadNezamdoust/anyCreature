A stylised bolt-action sniper rifle, one game weapon model. A very long heavy round dark gunmetal barrel with four shallow flutes, ending in a large box-shaped muzzle brake with two side ports. A slim round gunmetal receiver, and on top of it a long matte black telescopic scope on two ring mounts: a wide front bell, a narrow middle tube with two small round adjustment turrets (one on top, one on the left flank), and a narrower rear eyepiece; the lenses flat dark blue. A matte black polymer chassis stock: a straight fore-end under the rear third of the barrel, a vertical pistol grip with a gunmetal trigger inside a squared trigger guard, a short straight matte black box magazine in front of the guard, and a skeleton butt with a raised cheek rest and a flat butt pad. At the front of the fore-end a folded-down bipod: two thin gunmetal legs angled down and slightly forward, splayed left and right, each with a small flat foot. Safety-orange accents: the two scope adjustment turret caps, a band round the muzzle brake's rear end, and the two bipod feet. The bolt handle with its round knob and the ejection port are on the right flank, hidden in this view. About 1.20 m long, 0.09 m wide and 0.30 m tall.
