A stylised heavy double-action revolver, one game weapon model. A thick dark gunmetal frame with a flat top strap. A long heavy gunmetal barrel, slab-sided with a round bore, a full-length underlug beneath it and a ventilated rib with three slots along its top, a ramped blade front sight at the muzzle end and a boxy rear sight notch at the back of the top strap. In the middle of the frame a fat round gunmetal cylinder with six chambers and six rounded flutes on its outside, its axis parallel to the barrel. Behind the cylinder a spurred gunmetal hammer standing up at the rear of the frame. Under the frame a rounded trigger guard with a curved gunmetal trigger inside it. A matte black polymer grip raked backward with a finger-grooved front edge, a stippled side panel and a rounded base. Safety-orange accents: the front sight blade, the cylinder release latch on the left flank behind the cylinder, and a round medallion disc on the grip's side panel. The ejector rod tip shows under the barrel in front of the cylinder. The right flank has a plain side plate with one screw, hidden in this view. About 0.30 m long, 0.045 m wide and 0.16 m tall.
