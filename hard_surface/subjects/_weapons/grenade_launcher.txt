A stylised six-shot revolver grenade launcher, one game weapon model. In the middle a big dark gunmetal revolving cylinder, as wide as it is long, with six large round chambers around its axis and six shallow flutes on its outside. In front of the cylinder a short fat round gunmetal barrel with a thick muzzle ring, under the barrel a matte black polymer vertical fore grip. A slim dark gunmetal frame runs over the top of the cylinder from the barrel to the rear, carrying a short flat rail with a boxy reflex sight housing. Behind the cylinder a matte black polymer pistol grip raked backward with a gunmetal trigger inside a rounded trigger guard, and a matte black polymer skeleton stock: two thin tubes to a flat rubber butt pad. Safety-orange accents: a ring round the muzzle, the front face of the cylinder's centre pin cap, and the stock's adjustment latch on the left flank. The cylinder release lever is on the right flank, hidden in this view. About 0.78 m long, 0.16 m wide and 0.27 m tall.
