A stylised pump-action shotgun, one game weapon model. A long thick round dark gunmetal barrel with a small bead front sight on top near the muzzle, and directly under it a round gunmetal magazine tube almost as long as the barrel, the two joined near the muzzle by a flat clamp. Round the magazine tube a chunky matte black polymer pump fore-end with five deep vertical grip grooves on its side. A slab-sided dark gunmetal receiver with a flat top and a loading port cut in its underside. Behind the receiver a matte black polymer pistol grip raked backward, a gunmetal trigger inside a rounded trigger guard, and a fixed matte black polymer stock with a straight top line and a thick rubber butt pad. Safety-orange accents: the magazine tube's end cap at the muzzle, the bead front sight, and the cross-bolt safety button on the left flank behind the trigger. The ejection port is on the right flank, hidden in this view. About 1.00 m long, 0.06 m wide and 0.20 m tall.
