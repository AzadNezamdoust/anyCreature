A stylised belt-fed light machine gun, one game weapon model. A long boxy dark gunmetal receiver with a flat hinged feed cover on top carrying a short rail and a boxy rear sight. A long heavy round gunmetal barrel with a matte black carrying handle folded on top of it near the receiver, a triangular front sight tower near the muzzle, and a conical flash hider at the muzzle. Under the rear half of the barrel a matte black polymer handguard with four vent slots along its side. Under the receiver a matte black polymer pistol grip raked backward with a gunmetal trigger inside a rounded trigger guard. Hanging under the receiver in front of the grip a big squarish matte black ammunition box with a rounded bottom. A fixed matte black polymer stock with a hooked lower edge and a flat butt plate. Under the barrel in front of the handguard a folded-down bipod: two thin gunmetal legs angled down and slightly forward, splayed left and right, each with a small flat foot. Safety-orange accents: a band round the top edge of the ammunition box, the charging handle knob on the left flank of the receiver, and the two bipod feet. The belt feed tray opening with a short length of brass cartridge belt entering the receiver is on the left flank; the ejection port and the spent-link chute are on the right flank, hidden in this view. About 1.10 m long, 0.12 m wide and 0.30 m tall.
