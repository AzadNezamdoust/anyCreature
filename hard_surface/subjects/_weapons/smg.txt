A stylised compact submachine gun, one game weapon model. A short boxy dark gunmetal receiver with a flat rail along its top, a low rear sight notch block at the back and a hooded front sight post at the front. A short round gunmetal barrel sticking out of the front, wrapped by a stubby cylindrical barrel shroud with two rows of round vent holes. Under the front of the receiver a matte black polymer vertical fore grip. Under the middle a matte black polymer pistol grip raked backward with a gunmetal trigger inside a rounded trigger guard, and in front of the guard a long straight matte black stick magazine hanging straight down from a square magazine well. At the rear a matte black folding wire stock extended straight back: two thin parallel rods to a small flat butt plate. Safety-orange accents: the magazine's floor plate, the charging handle knob on the left flank of the receiver, and the front sight hood. A small gunmetal fire selector lever on the left flank above the grip. The ejection port is on the right flank, hidden in this view. About 0.62 m long, 0.06 m wide and 0.28 m tall.
