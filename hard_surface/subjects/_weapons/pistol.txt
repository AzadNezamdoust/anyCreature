A stylised semi-automatic pistol, one game weapon model. A long slab-sided dark gunmetal slide with a flat top, six slanted grip serrations at its rear end on the side, a blocky rear sight and a small blade front sight on top, and the round muzzle of the barrel showing in its front face above a recoil spring plug. Under the slide a matte black polymer frame with a short accessory rail under the muzzle end, a squared trigger guard with a gunmetal trigger inside it, and a thick grip raked backward with a stippled panel on its side and a flared base. The magazine's floor plate closes the bottom of the grip. Safety-orange accents: the magazine's floor plate, the front sight blade, and the round magazine release button on the left flank behind the trigger guard. A small gunmetal slide stop lever and a thumb safety lever on the left flank of the frame. The ejection port is on the right flank and top of the slide, hidden in this view. About 0.21 m long, 0.035 m wide and 0.15 m tall.
