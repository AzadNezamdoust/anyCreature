A stylised hand-carried rotary minigun, one game weapon model. At the front a cluster of six long round dark gunmetal barrels arranged in a circle around one axis, held together by two flat round clamp discs, one at the muzzle end and one halfway along. Behind the barrels a fat cylindrical dark gunmetal motor and receiver housing with a flat rear cap. On top of the housing a matte black polymer carrying handle shaped like a long flat arch running front to back. At the rear a matte black polymer spade grip: a vertical D-shaped handle with a gunmetal trigger paddle inside it. Under the housing a big round matte black ammunition drum lying on its side, its flat faces facing left and right, joined to the housing by a short gunmetal feed chute at its front. Safety-orange accents: the front barrel clamp disc, a band round the rim of the ammunition drum, and the trigger paddle. The motor's power cable socket and the feed chute latch are on the right flank, hidden in this view. About 1.05 m long, 0.24 m wide and 0.42 m tall.
