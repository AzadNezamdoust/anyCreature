A stylised assault rifle, one game weapon model. A long dark gunmetal receiver with a flat rail along its whole top, a boxy rear sight block at the back end of the rail and a triangular front sight tower above the barrel near the muzzle. A straight round gunmetal barrel sticking out of the handguard and ending in a chunky cylindrical muzzle brake with two side slots. A matte black polymer handguard, octagonal in section, wrapping the rear two thirds of the barrel, with three oval vent slots along its side. Under the receiver a matte black polymer pistol grip raked backward, a gunmetal trigger inside a rounded black trigger guard, and in front of the guard a curved matte black box magazine hanging down and forward from a square magazine well. A fixed matte black polymer stock, a straight tube from the receiver to a tall flat butt plate with a triangular lower strut. Safety-orange accents: the magazine's floor plate, a small charging handle knob on the left flank of the receiver, and a thin band round the front end of the handguard. A small gunmetal fire selector lever on the left flank above the grip. The ejection port and the bolt release are on the right flank, hidden in this view. About 0.90 m long, 0.07 m wide and 0.26 m tall.
