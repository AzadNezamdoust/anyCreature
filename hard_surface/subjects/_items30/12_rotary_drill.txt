A fictional heavy two-hand rotary mining drill game prop for a tunnel breaker, held level with the bit forward. A big brick-red painted shell motor housing with large chamfers, cylindrical at its front, drives a cone-shaped dark steel drill bit with three spiral flutes and a blunt point, the cone 0.45 m long and 0.30 m across at its base. Around the base of the bit a wide charcoal rubber dust collar ring. At the rear a charcoal closed D-handle with a trigger; on top of the housing a charcoal arched handle running front to back; a charcoal side handle sticks out of the left side. A tiny teal lamp window on the left flank. The right flank carries a matching charcoal side handle and a round brick gearbox cover. Held with two hands at chest height. About 1.00 m long, 0.42 m wide, 0.42 m tall.
