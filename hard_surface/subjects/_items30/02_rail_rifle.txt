A fictional long precision game rifle prop for a mining scout, held level with the muzzle forward. The forward half is two parallel flat dark steel rails, stacked with a clear 25 mm gap, joined at the tip by a square dark steel muzzle block and at the rear by an olive painted shell housing with large chamfers. The housing carries three raised dark steel coil rings around its front end and a slim charcoal scope tube on top. Behind it a diagonal-ribbed charcoal pistol grip and a flat-sided olive shoulder stock with a charcoal butt pad. A short charcoal cell pack plugs in under the housing. A tiny teal lamp window on the left flank above the cell pack. The right flank carries a charcoal charge lever and a sling loop. Held with two hands at the shoulder. About 1.15 m long, 0.07 m wide, 0.26 m tall.
