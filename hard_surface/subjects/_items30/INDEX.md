## Family rules

**Shell colour per class** (painted shell panels only; everything else is charcoal or dark steel):

| Class | Shell family | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Breaker | muted brick red-brown (rust brick) | heavy, boxy, the biggest volumes |
| Scout | muted olive | slim, long, light |
| Engineer | muted ochre / tan | tanks, drums, blocky heads |
| Warden | muted slate blue-grey | rounded bodies, tanks and hoses |
| Any class | neutral sand-grey | plain utility gear |

**Shared across all 30:**
- Materials: three only. Matte charcoal polymer (grips, handles, straps, hoses, pads, racks), dark steel (barrels, bits, nozzles, springs, hooks, spools, feet), painted shell in the class colour (main housing). No wear, no decals, no text.
- Chamfers: slightly oversized 45° chamfers on every shell edge, about 8-12 mm on handhelds and 15-25 mm on worn and placed items.
- The family grip: one diagonal-ribbed charcoal pistol grip with a squared trigger guard. Two-hand items add a charcoal vertical front handle with three ribs, a charcoal arched top carry handle, or both.
- Lamp: exactly one tiny teal rectangular lamp window per item (a dot on pistols and charges), on the left flank or front face. No other lamp colours: orange-red, green, gold, blue, magenta and amber are reserved game colours.
- Straps and fittings: flat charcoal webbing with rectangular buckles on everything worn or carried.
- Effects off: no flame, beam, glow, dome or spark is drawn; heat and energy read from hardware (pilot tube, ceramic rings, emitter rings, nozzle bells).
- View: front = working end (muzzle, bit, nozzle, hook, striker); the left flank faces the viewer. Every brief names the hidden right flank (or back / underside) content.
- Worn and placed items appear alone: no person, no ground.
- Fictional props only: shapes and colours, no real firearm makes or model names.
