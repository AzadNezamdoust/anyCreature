A fictional rivet-firing pistol game prop for a demolition engineer, shaped like a heavy construction fastening tool, held level with the nose forward. A tall ochre painted shell head with large chamfers ends at the front in a flat square dark steel nose plate with a short round dark steel firing tube in its centre. Behind the head a diagonal-ribbed charcoal pistol grip and a long squared trigger guard. A slim straight dark steel rivet magazine runs at a slant from under the nose back to the grip base, showing a row of round rivet heads along its side. A round charcoal air-cell cap closes the top rear of the head. A tiny teal lamp window on the left flank of the head. The right flank carries a charcoal belt hook on a plain ochre panel. Held in one hand. About 0.30 m long, 0.07 m wide, 0.24 m tall.
