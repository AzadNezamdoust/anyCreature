A fictional signal flare pistol game prop for an underground crew, held level with the muzzle forward. A short very wide sand-grey painted shell barrel, 40 mm bore with a thick chamfered rim, hinged under its rear to a charcoal frame so it breaks open downward, shown closed. A big dark steel hammer spur on top at the rear. A diagonal-ribbed charcoal pistol grip and a large round trigger guard sized for gloves, a charcoal lanyard ring at the grip base. A flat charcoal sight ridge along the top of the barrel. One tiny teal lamp dot on the left flank of the frame. The right flank carries a charcoal side clip holding three short fat spare flare shells with dark steel bases. Held in one hand. About 0.28 m long, 0.07 m wide (0.10 m with the clip), 0.18 m tall.
