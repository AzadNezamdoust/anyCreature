A fictional set of sticky breaching charges game prop for a demolition engineer: three identical charges seated in an open charcoal carry rack. Each charge is a squat ochre painted shell disc 0.20 m across and 0.08 m thick with large chamfered edges, a shallow domed top with a raised ring around its rim and a short raised arrow moulding pointing at the centre, and a charcoal rubber adhesive pad on its underside. A small round dark steel fuse cap sits at the centre of each dome with a tiny teal lamp dot beside it. The rack is a flat charcoal tray with three round cups and an arched handle across its length. The hidden back of the rack carries a belt clip on a plain charcoal wall. Each charge is thrown with one hand. Rack about 0.66 m long, 0.22 m wide, 0.16 m tall.
