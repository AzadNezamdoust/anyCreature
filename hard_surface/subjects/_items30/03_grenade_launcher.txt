A fictional short heavy grenade-throwing game weapon prop for a demolition engineer, held level with the muzzle forward. A single wide dark steel launch tube, 50 mm bore with a thick chamfered rim, sits over a large revolving drum in ochre painted shell with four big round chambers visible on its front face. Behind the drum a boxy ochre frame with large chamfers, a diagonal-ribbed charcoal pistol grip, and a charcoal folding shoulder stock made of two flat struts and a pad. Under the tube ahead of the drum a charcoal vertical handle with three ribs. A simple flat charcoal ladder sight on top of the tube. A tiny teal lamp window on the left side of the frame. The right flank carries the drum's release latch and a hinged charcoal loading gate. Held with two hands at the shoulder. About 0.78 m long, 0.16 m wide, 0.28 m tall.
