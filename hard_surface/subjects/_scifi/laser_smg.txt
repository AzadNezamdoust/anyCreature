A cartoon science-fiction rapid laser gun, one game weapon model. A compact rounded light grey body with a short barrel made of three thin mid grey tubes bundled in a triangle, held by a bright red ring at the muzzle and another where they meet the body. Under the body in front of the grip a straight clear glass battery stick pointing down, filled flat bright yellow, with a mid grey foot cap. A dark teal pistol grip raked backward with a bright red trigger in a rounded mid grey guard. On top a low off-white rounded cover with a small upright bright red sight fin at the front and a mid grey notch block at the rear. At the rear a thin mid grey wire stock: one bent rod to a small flat off-white butt pad. A bright yellow selector dial is on the right flank, hidden in this view. About 0.52 m long, 0.08 m wide and 0.26 m tall.
