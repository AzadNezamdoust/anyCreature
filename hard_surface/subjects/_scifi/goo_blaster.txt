A cartoon science-fiction goo blaster, one game weapon model. A short wide off-white body shaped like a rounded bean. At the front a very wide short mid grey barrel with a thick rolled bright green rim. On top a tall upright clear glass jar tank with a flat mid grey screw lid, filled flat bright slime green. Under the body at the front a round mid grey pump handle on a short slide rod, and behind it a chunky dark purple pistol grip with a bright yellow trigger in a rounded mid grey guard. At the rear a round off-white cap with a bright yellow pressure release button in its centre. Two riveted mid grey bands wrap the body, one in front of the jar and one behind. A bright yellow crank handle is on the right flank, hidden in this view. About 0.48 m long, 0.18 m wide and 0.38 m tall.
