A cartoon science-fiction ray pistol, one game weapon model. A fat rounded off-white body like a short egg lying on its side, with a mid grey tapered emitter tube at the front ending in three stacked mid grey rings of shrinking size and a small bright red ball tip. On top of the body a clear glass dome showing flat bright lime-green fluid. At the rear of the body a round mid grey end cap with a small bright yellow dial. Underneath, a chunky dark teal pistol grip raked backward with an oversized bright red trigger inside a rounded mid grey trigger guard. Two small off-white tail fins, one on each flank at the rear. A small bright yellow power switch is on the right flank, hidden in this view. About 0.30 m long, 0.09 m wide and 0.20 m tall.
