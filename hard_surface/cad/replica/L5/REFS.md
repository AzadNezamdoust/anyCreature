# L5 replica refs - Porsche 911 Carrera S (992.1) coupe

Images saved (git-ignored) in `hard_surface/cad/refs/L5/`; `cand/` = 640 px candidates screened on a contact sheet.
No free 992 orthographic blueprint was found (the-blueprints.com / getoutlines only list 991 or sell paid files), so
near-orthographic telephoto photos are used, registered to spec dimensions (method in refdata.py docstring).
